# 🏀 LeBonBon: "Grow Up to be LeBron" - Vision Board

## 🌟 The Core Philosophy
A simple, hyper-addictive gameplay loop that effortlessly transitions from an active clicking game into a satisfying idle/management experience. Inspired by the frictionless loop of games like *Sell Lemons*.

---

## 🔄 The Core Loop
1. **Click to Shoot:** The player starts by manually shooting the ball to earn their first bits of Cash.
2. **Hire Roster (Gacha/Roll):** They spend Cash to "Roll" for NPC shooters. Because stats and attributes (Slasher, Limitless Range) are randomized, every roll has the excitement of a slot machine.
3. **Automate & Optimize:** The NPCs shoot autonomously. The player drags and places them optimally on the court to maximize their specific distance-based attributes.
4. **Scale Up:** Cash generation snowballs. The player buys better Hoop Upgrades (global multipliers) and rolls for higher-tier players.
5. **Rebirth (Prestige):** When progress slows, the player Rebirths, wiping their court but gaining a permanent multiplier.

---

## 🎣 The "Hooks" (Long-Term Retention)

### 1. The Chase for the "Perfect Player" (God Rolls & Shinies)
*   **Must-Have:** Rolling players needs extreme variance to make hitting the jackpot feel amazing.
*   *Mechanic:* Introduce incredibly rare "Shiny" or "God Roll" shooters that have glowing auras and massively boosted base yields or impossibly fast cooldowns. 

### 2. Inventory Management & Merging
*   **Inventory Limit:** Players can only hold a certain number of shooters on their bench. This forces them to manage their roster.
*   **Merge Feature:** To prevent the inventory limit from feeling punishing, players can merge or fuse low-tier shooters together to upgrade them into higher-tier players, or use them as "fuel" to boost the stats of a Star Player.

### 3. "Off the Court" Earnings (Bench Player Utility)
*   **The Vision:** Just like the real LeBron James, most of the money eventually shouldn't come from just shooting hoops—it should come from businesses and brand deals!
*   *Mechanic:* Players can assign their "Benched" inventory players to various Off-Court Businesses (e.g., Shoe Deals, Taco Tuesday Franchises, Movie Deals). The stats of the benched players dictate how much passive income those businesses generate.

### 4. Cosmetic Milestone Unlocks
*   **Progression Rewards:** Reaching specific Rebirths or Cash Milestones doesn't just make the numbers go up—it unlocks cool cosmetics.
*   *Examples:* Equipping different colored ball trails, unlocking unique swish/clang sound effects, custom backboards, or crazy jump-shot animations.
