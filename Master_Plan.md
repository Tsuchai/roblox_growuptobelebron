# 🏀 LeBonBon: Master Plan & Feature Tracker

This document exists to prevent **Feature Creep**. When a new idea pops up, we log it here. If it doesn't fit the "Core Tycoon Loop", it goes into the Icebox.

## 🌟 The Core Identity
A **Tycoon/Idle Game** mixed with **NBA GM Simulation**. 
Players roll for shooters, arrange them on a court, and make money. The depth comes from the **Gacha Variance** (Star Levels) and **Roster Management** (Merging/Trading), *not* from overly complex micro-management.

---

## ✅ Phase 1: The Core Loop (IMPLEMENTED)
- [x] **Procedural Courts:** Players spawn into their own plot.
- [x] **Autonomous Shooting:** NPCs shoot automatically based on Cooldown.
- [x] **Dynamic Math:** Cash earned is based on Distance to Hoop + Attribute Multipliers.
- [x] **Gacha Rolling:** Spending cash to roll for random Tiers (Rookie -> LeBron).
- [x] **Star Rarity System:** A newly rolled player is assigned 1 to 5 Stars (IVs/Potential), massively boosting their starting stats.
- [x] **Inventory Limits:** Players are capped at 30 shooters to encourage roster management.
- [x] **Global Draft Event:** A special UI draft that opens for exactly 60 seconds every 5 minutes globally.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 2: Refinement & UI (CURRENT FOCUS)
- [ ] **Visual Polish:** Ensure the UI (Shop, Roster) clearly communicates the 1 to 5 Star ratings.
- [ ] **The Trade Block:** Allowing players to package 3 shooters together for random NPC GM counter-offers.
- [ ] **Monetization Hooks:** Adding Robux prompts (like Stealing a Draft Pick, or bypassing the 5-minute wait).

---

## 🧊 The Icebox (Ideas for the Future)
*These are great ideas, but we are setting them aside until Phase 1 and 2 are 100% polished and fun.*

1. **The XP / Leveling System:** Allowing shooters to gain XP per shot and level up from Lv 1 to Lv 10. *(Status: Paused to keep the game simple).*
2. **Off-Court Businesses:** Using "benched" players to generate passive income (Shoe Deals, Taco Tuesday).
3. **Playoff Boss Fights:** Simulating a game against an NPC team every few hours for a Championship Ring.
4. **Team Chemistry:** Giving shooters stat boosts if they are placed next to compatible teammates (e.g., a Slasher next to a Playmaker).
